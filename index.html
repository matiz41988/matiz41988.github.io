    Slow mornings, the way they used to be

    Rewind started with a simple idea: the best part of the day is the bit where nobody is rushing. So we built a café that feels like that.

    We pull every shot with care, bake before we open and know most of our regulars by their order. Whether you are meeting a friend, getting some work done or just need a good flat white, there is a seat here for you.
